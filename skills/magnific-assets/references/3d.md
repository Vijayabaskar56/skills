# 3D: models, scenes and 360 images

Magnific lists 3D Generator, 3D Scenes, 3D Editor and 360 Generator. Whether any of them is free
in Relax mode was not verified: open the tool, turn Relax on, and read the Generate button. If it
shows a credit number, stop and tell the person; do not generate.

## Inputs

- Start from the 2D art when there is one, so the model keeps its shapes and colours.
- Say what the asset is for: a turntable render, a still from a fixed angle, or a model the app
  loads. A still or a short turntable is usually cheaper to ship than a model.

## After generating

1. Download the model in the format the project loads (check what the project already ships), or
   render the stills and turntable frames it needs.
2. For a turntable or animated render, export frames and treat them like a clip: contact sheet,
   `judging.md`, then `encode.sh` and `audit.py` as for video.
3. Check the silhouette and colours at card size on light and dark, next to the 2D set.

Judging: same shapes and palette as the 2D art, no melted or missing parts from the back side,
clean edges, and within the size budget.
